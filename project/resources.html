Resources & Emergency Hotlines

Keep these numbers saved. In a life-threatening emergency, call the police immediately.

Nigeria Police Force

Emergency: 199 or 112

National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA)

Contact via state emergency numbers or official NEMA channels.

Lagos State Emergency

767 or 112

Domestic Violence & Gender-Based Support

Search for verified state or NGO hotlines in your area. Many operate 24/7.

Additional Guidance

  • Always prioritise your personal safety before attempting to help others.
  • Share your live location with a trusted contact when travelling.
  • Report suspicious activity to the nearest police station or through official channels.
  • Verify any security information from trusted government or community sources.

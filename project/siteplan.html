🛡️ SafeNaija

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

SafeNaija

Why this name? “SafeNaija” combines “Safe” with the popular affectionate term “Naija” for Nigeria. It is short, memorable, positive, and clearly communicates the goal of improving safety and security across the country. It feels approachable for everyday Nigerians while remaining serious enough for the topic of insecurity.

Optional domain idea: safenaija.org or safenaija.com.ng

2. Site Purpose

SafeNaija is a community-focused platform dedicated to combating insecurity in Nigeria. The site serves as a practical hub that provides:

  • Security tips and best practices for individuals, families, and communities
  • Clear information on how and where to report suspicious activities or crimes
  • Regional security alerts and updates
  • Resources for victims of insecurity (hotlines, support organizations, emergency contacts)
  • Community success stories and local initiatives that promote peace and vigilance
  • Educational content on recognizing threats and building safer neighborhoods

The overall goal is to empower ordinary Nigerians with knowledge and tools so they can contribute to a safer nation.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent typical concerns of the target audience (concerned citizens, parents, community leaders, and young adults):

  • Scenario 1: “I noticed some unusual movements in my area at night. How can I safely report this without putting myself at risk?”
  • Scenario 2: “What practical steps can my family take to stay safer when traveling between states or during festive periods?”
  • Additional scenario (bonus): “Are there community watch groups or local security initiatives I can join in my local government area?”

4. Color Schema

The palette draws inspiration from the Nigerian flag (green for hope and agriculture) combined with dark navy for seriousness and trust, plus a warm gold accent for calls-to-action and important alerts.

Primary – Deep Green (#006400)
Used for: Main headings, navigation links, important buttons, section borders, and logo.

Secondary – Dark Navy (#1a1a2e)
Used for: Body text, footer background, strong contrast elements.

Accent – Warm Gold/Orange (#f4a261)
Used for: Call-to-action buttons, important highlights, alert badges, and underlines.

Background – Light Gray (#f8f9fa) + White for content cards.

5. Typography

Two fonts are used for clarity and professionalism:

  • Headings: Georgia / Times New Roman (serif)
    Gives a formal, trustworthy, and serious tone suitable for a security-focused site.
  • Body text & navigation: Segoe UI / system-ui (sans-serif)
    Highly readable on screens of all sizes and modern in appearance.

No more than these two primary fonts will be used across the site for consistency.
